Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

QuestLife lets you plan activities, record quests and memories, and connect with friends. This policy explains the information used to make those features work, when it can be seen by others, and how to manage or delete it. You can contact us at support@questlife.app.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the QuestLife mobile app, web app, and public website. QuestLife is personally operated from Florida, United States; “QuestLife,” “we,” and “us” refer to that operator. It does not govern independent sites or services you choose to open from QuestLife, including app stores and external map links.

We collect information you give us, information generated when you use a feature, and limited technical information needed to operate and protect the service. The exact data depends on the features and permissions you choose.

2. Information we handle

  • Account and profile: email address, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, sign-in provider identity when you use Apple or Google, username, first and last name, display name, biography, and profile images. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Quest and journal activity: saved quests and collections, progress, completions, streaks, points, ratings, reflections, activity notes, titles, photos, and other content you create. Some drafts and active quest records are kept on your device and synchronized to your account when possible.
  • Social activity: friend and follow relationships, invitations, shared quest posts, comments, reactions, challenges, and the audience selected for posts and profile details.
  • Device and service information: push notification token and time zone if you enable push notifications, notification preferences, device and operating-system details, security and access logs, and crash diagnostics when diagnostic reporting is configured.

Our public website is a static site. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, and visit time for delivery and security. The web app stores session and local draft data in browser storage. We have not added advertising cookies or an advertising SDK to QuestLife.

3. Location, camera, and photos

If you choose route recording for an active quest, QuestLife uses your device location to draw and save your route, measure distance, and restore progress. Route points may include coordinates, time, and accuracy information. They are stored on your device and may sync to your private account record. With background location permission, recording can continue while the app is in the background during an active quest. Operating-system limits can interrupt it. The map may also use a recent device location when you open it with location permission.

QuestLife does not need location access for every feature. You can pause or stop route recording in the app and change location permissions in your device settings. Turning permission off stops new location collection by the app but does not automatically erase a route already saved; delete the associated data or your account to request removal.

When you choose to take or select a photo, the app accesses the camera or selected image to add it to a quest, journal entry, collection, profile, or post. Quest photos and drafts may be stored locally before upload. Photos you publish are visible to the audience you selected. Saving a copy to your device photo library is optional.

4. Contacts and finding friends

If you choose “Connect contacts” on a supported device, QuestLife reads email addresses from your contacts and sends those addresses to our service to look for matching QuestLife accounts. The app uses the matches to show people you may know. We do not use the address book to send invitations automatically. You can use username search or a QR code without granting contact access.

The contact lookup function matches addresses against account emails and returns matching profiles; it does not create an address-book table. The request may still be processed in normal service and security logs. You can revoke contact permission in device settings.

5. How we use information

We use account data to create and secure your account; quest, journal, and location data to provide the features you choose; social data to connect people and show content to the selected audience; notification data to deliver the alerts you enable; and technical data to maintain, debug, and protect the service. We may use aggregated information to understand and improve reliability without identifying individual members in the results.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, our bases may include performing the service you request, your choice to enable optional device features, our legitimate interests in security and reliability, and legal obligations. You can withdraw device permissions or turn off notifications without deleting your account.

6. When information is shared

Other QuestLife members. Your username, public profile details, and content you publish can be seen according to the feature’s visibility settings. Your biography is public. Friend, follow, and post audiences can differ. Journal reflections are private unless you deliberately share material from them. Review the audience before sharing a post. People who can view content may copy it outside the app.

Service providers. Supabase handles account authentication, database records, file storage, and related backend operations. Expo’s push service and device-platform providers help deliver enabled notifications. Sentry may process crash diagnostics if reporting is configured; the app is set to remove account, request, screenshot, and breadcrumb data from diagnostic events. Vercel hosts the public website. Apple, Google, and map providers may process information under their own terms when you use their sign-in, app-store, or map services. These providers receive data only as needed for their roles.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably needed to investigate abuse, protect people, enforce our Terms, or protect the service. We do not sell member data or use it for cross-app advertising in the current product.

7. Your controls

You can edit profile details and visibility in the app, choose the audience for a social post, remove content where an edit or delete option is offered, and change notification preferences. Device settings let you revoke location, camera, photo, contacts, and notification permissions. You can sign out to clear account-scoped local data on that device. If you use the web app, clearing browser storage removes local sessions and drafts from that browser, but does not delete server records.

Some service messages, such as account verification or password reset, are necessary to operate your account and are separate from optional push notifications.

8. How long data is kept

Account information and content you keep in QuestLife remain while your account is active or until you delete specific content. Local active quest drafts may remain on your device while an activity is unfinished or recovering. Contact emails used for a lookup are not added to a persistent address-book table. Notification device registrations can be deactivated when you sign out or change settings.

When you delete an account, our deletion process removes the account and associated records and attempts to remove account-owned uploaded media. Limited records may remain for a period in backups, security logs, or records we must keep for legal or operational reasons; retained audit records are designed to have account identifiers removed. We keep these only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and then delete or de-identify them. Content that other people copied outside QuestLife is beyond our control.

9. Delete your QuestLife account

In the app or web app, go to Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. This permanently removes access to the account and starts deletion of its associated data. If you cannot access the app, email support@questlife.app with “QuestLife Account Deletion” in the subject. We will verify account ownership before processing a request made by email.

Deleting an account is different from uninstalling the app or signing out. Uninstalling only removes the app from that device; signing out ends the local session. If an account deletion fails, contact us so we can investigate and complete it.

10. Access and privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, download, or delete personal information; object to or limit certain uses; withdraw consent; and appeal or complain to a privacy regulator. To make a request, email support@questlife.app. We may ask you to verify your identity before giving access to or changing account information. We will respond as required by applicable law.

QuestLife’s current product does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If this practice changes, we will update this policy and provide any legally required choices before doing so.

11. Children

QuestLife requires users to be at least 13 and does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Older minors should use QuestLife with a parent or guardian’s permission when required by local law.

12. Security, location of processing, and changes

We use access controls, authenticated accounts, and other reasonable safeguards to protect information. No internet or storage system is perfectly secure. Data may be processed in the United States and other places where our providers operate; protections may differ by location.

We may update this policy as QuestLife changes. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice for material changes when appropriate. Review this page from time to time.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, email support@questlife.app.
